Share a post from Instagram, TikTok or Facebook, or a Google Maps place or list. We fetch it, save the photos and videos as our own files, and show them back from our copy.

Connect this page to the runner

Requests are sent by saving a small file to the repository, which starts the scraper in GitHub Actions. That needs a GitHub fine-grained token with Contents: Read and write on only. It is stored in this browser and nowhere else.

Use it like an app

Android (Chrome): tap Install app above, or the browser menu → Install app. After that, "Taste" appears in the share menu of Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Google Maps (Share → more): pick it and the link is fetched straight away.

iPhone: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. iPhone web apps can't appear in the share menu, so add this one-tap Shortcut for sharing:

  1. Open Shortcuts, tap +, name it Send to Taste.
  2. Tap the ⓘ button, turn on Show in Share Sheet, and set it to receive URLs and Text.
  3. Add the action URL Encode (input: Shortcut Input).
  4. Add Text: followed by the URL Encoded Text variable.
  5. Add Open URLs (input: that Text).
  6. In Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or Google Maps: Share → (more) → Send to Taste.

The Shortcut opens Safari, which keeps its own copy of the access key, so paste your token there once too.

From your saved items

Saved collections, favourites and private lists are only visible to their owner, so we can't open them without logging in (which we never do). Download your data instead and drop the ZIP, JSON or CSV here. It is read in your browser; only the links are sent.

  • Instagram: Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information → Saved, format JSON.
  • TikTok: Settings → Account → Download your data, format JSON (favourite and liked videos).
  • Facebook: Accounts Center → Export your information → Saved items and collections, format JSON.
  • Google Maps: Google Takeout → Saved (one CSV per list) and Maps (your places). A list you've shared can simply be pasted in Share a link.
