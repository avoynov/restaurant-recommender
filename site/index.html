From your saved collections

Instagram only shows saved collections to their owner, so we can't open a collection link without logging in (which we never do). Instead, download your data from Instagram (Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information → Saved, format JSON) and drop the ZIP or saved_collections.json / saved_posts.json here. It is read in your browser; only the post links are sent.
